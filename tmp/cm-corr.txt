docs: correct what the 48 attempts_exhausted blocks mean

Section 1 originally said the cards were "failing on their own merits" and that
supply of tractable cards limited the run. Both are wrong, so both are struck
through in place and replaced with 1.1.

What the evidence actually shows: attempts_exhausted is the L4 rung of the
recovery ladder (recover.py:121), which fires at attempt >= 3 where that counter
accumulates across runs. The 48 blocks are 16 cards x 3 blocks each, and none of
those cards got a single AGENT_STARTED in this window. Across their whole
history those 16 cards show 20 agents that started and never exited, 10
AGENT_FAILED (9 UNKNOWN, 1 NO_CHANGES), 6 INFRASTRUCTURE_FAILED, 4 that ever
reached the oracle gate, and 0 ever integrated.

So neither the models nor the cards failed. No model-side failure class exists
anywhere in the ledger: every AGENT_FAILED ever recorded is UNKNOWN or
NO_CHANGES, with no QUOTA, AUTH, MODEL_GONE or CRASH. The dominant signature is
agents cut off when their parent run ended - 38 across the whole ledger, 115
started against 77 exited - which is the same pathology already documented in
9.2: overrunning runs, one tick of 3399.9s, and the REC-15 NameError crash that
killed five agents mid-flight.

The consequence matters more than the correction. Those counters were spent by
aborted runs and then inherited by the private copy, so the fleet refused cards
that were never tried in this arm. The low width is largely an artifact of a
poisoned card pool, not evidence that a cap of 10 fails to bind - which is what
the original wording implied. Testing the cap needs attempt counters reset and
both arms re-run; that is a further intervention beyond the single requeue D-79
allows per arm and is left to the operator.
