Verdict on D-78: two questions, one answered. Width is answered positively -
cap 10 sustained exactly 10 concurrent agents and never 11, holding 10 for
92.4% of a 6612s window; cap 20 sustained 20 and held it for 56.2% of a 1550s
window. Raising the cap doubled sustained width. Throughput is not answered:
landed was flat at 4.900 vs 4.645 INTEGRATED/h while produced rose 4.50x from
9.800 to 44.129 SUBMITTED/h, leaving a terminal backlog of 17 submitted to 2
landed at cap 20 against 0.9:1 at cap 10. Three reasons it is unanswerable
here: windows differed 4.3x in opposite directions, supply ran out in both
arms at exactly 25 claims, and the limiter moved to integration mid-run.
